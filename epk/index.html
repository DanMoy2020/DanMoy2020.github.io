Bio

Sheffields finest (and first) 'lovesick loser' band.

Their newest single Lost at Sea offers a sage and candid approach to heartbreak that, much like gaggle.’s eagerly-anticipated upcoming album A Glass Half Drunk , is punctuated by tongue-in-cheek commentary and is set against a backdrop of pints, pubs, and youthful optimism – a young, fresh take on the kitchen sink realism pioneered by Sheffield forefathers Arctic Monkeys and Pulp.

In the year that gaggle. have been together, the five members of gaggle. (alongside goose mascot Bernie) have become a staple of the Sheffield live-music scene, performing at grassroots venues all over the city and beyond to fans looking for witty observations on love, loss, and (almost) alcoholism.

Look out for the single’s B-side Stella Eater for a further taste of gaggle.’s penchant for beer and social scrutiny, and follow gaggle. on Instagram for more details and exclusive behind-the-scenes videos on the production of A Glass Half Drunk.
